About

Working across research, code, and narrative.

My work sits at the intersection of digital culture, science and society, and human-centered technology. I write about how systems form our habits, how institutions shape opportunity, and how we can build more humane tools.

I’ve worked in public-interest technology, research labs, and editorial environments, and I continue to write on the relationship between technical systems and everyday life.
