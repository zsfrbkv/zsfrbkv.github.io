Less is more.

- start with a message: what should a reader understand from the plot?
- avoid complicated plots –– split your messages into simple chunks
- spell out as much legend in the plot itself as possible
- use consistent colours throughout to tell a story. do not use the same colours to show different things
- consider colour interpretation
    - red/green as "bad"/"good"
    - national distinctions (red = good for some countries)
    - grey as neutral/background
    - blue/yellow as two opposite outcomes
    - light green as low amount and dark as high amount (e.g. hypomethylation and hypermethylation)
    - blue for men pink for women
- prioritise simplicity over aesthetics: does this add information or is this a pure stylistic choice? (eg matrix with/without borders)
- make sure colours are readable and easy to distinguish
- consider colour blindness
